**Last Updated:** November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Just Read** (`com.justread.app`, "we", "our", or "the App") handles your information.

## 1. Overview & Local-Only Processing

**Just Read** is designed as an offline-first PDF reader. In its current release:

- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal information, reading history, or analytics to external servers.
- **Local File Processing:** All PDF documents you open or import are processed strictly locally on your Android device. Your files are never uploaded to our servers.
- **No Third-Party Tracking or Ads:** The App does not include advertising SDKs or third-party behavioral trackers.

## 2. Permissions

Just Read only accesses files and storage locations you explicitly select via the Android system file picker or local app storage to render and organize your PDFs.

## 3. Future Cloud & Optional Features (Planned)

If you choose to use optional cloud features in a future update (such as Firebase Authentication for cross-device preference sync, Firebase Crashlytics for anonymous crash diagnostics, or third-party open-library catalogs):

- **Account Information:** Only the authentication identifiers (e.g., email address and Firebase UID) required to sign you in will be collected.
- **Synced Preferences:** Bookmarks, reading progress, and app settings will be stored securely in Google Firebase solely to sync across your devices.
- **Diagnostics:** Anonymous crash logs may be collected via Firebase Crashlytics to improve app stability.

This Privacy Policy will be updated before any cloud or diagnostic SDKs are enabled in a public release.

## 4. Data Deletion

Because the current version stores all data locally on your device, uninstalling the App or clearing its app storage removes all local app data.

Once optional cloud accounts are introduced, you will be able to delete your account and synced cloud data at any time using the instructions on our [Account & Data Deletion page](/delete-account/).

## 5. Children's Privacy

Just Read does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).
