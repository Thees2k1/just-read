---
title: Getting Started
description: How to open and read PDFs in Just Read on Android.
---

Welcome to **Just Read**! Follow these steps to install and start reading your documents:

1. **Download & Install:** Grab the latest signed APK from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/Thees2k1/just-read/releases) (direct link: [just-read.apk (v0.1.0-alpha.1)](https://github.com/Thees2k1/just-read/releases/download/v0.1.0-alpha.1/just-read.apk)) and allow installing from your browser/files app when prompted by Android.
2. **Open the App:** Launch **Just Read** on your Android device — an 8-page Quick Guide is preloaded so you can try scrolling, themes, and zoom right away.
3. **Add a PDF:** Tap the **+** button to pick any PDF file from your device storage.
4. **Read Comfortably:** Scroll continuously through pages with automatic Dragonite light/dark theme support matching your system settings.
