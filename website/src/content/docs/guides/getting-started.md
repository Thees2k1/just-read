---
title: Getting Started
description: How to open and read PDFs in Just Read on Android.
---

Welcome to **Just Read**! Follow these steps to start reading your documents:

1. **Open the App:** Launch **Just Read** on your Android device.
2. **Add a PDF:** Tap the add button to pick a PDF file from your device storage.
3. **Read Comfortably:** Swipe or scroll through pages with automatic light/dark theme support matching your system settings.
