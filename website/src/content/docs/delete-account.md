title: Account & Data Deletion
description: How to delete your Just Read account and associated cloud data.
---

> **Status Note:** The current release of **Just Read** (`com.justread.app`) operates offline-only and does not require or create user accounts. All reading data is stored locally on your device and can be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the App.
>
> The instructions below apply once optional **Cloud Sync (Firebase Authentication)** is enabled.

**Just Read** respects your right to control your personal data. When cloud accounts are active, you can permanently delete your account and synced cloud data using either method below.

## Option 1: Delete Directly in the App

1. Open **Just Read** on your Android device.
2. Go to **Settings > Account**.
3. Tap **Delete Account** and confirm your choice.

## Option 2: Request Deletion via Web / Email (If App is Uninstalled)

If you no longer have the app installed on your device:

1. Send an email to **`tongnguyenthe@gmail.com`** from the email address registered with your account.
2. Use the subject line: `Account Deletion Request - Just Read`.
3. We will verify your request and permanently delete your cloud data within **30 days**.

## What Data Is Deleted?

When your deletion request is processed, the following data is permanently removed from our Firebase servers:

- Your Firebase Authentication profile (UID and email address).
- All synced reading preferences, bookmarks, and reading progress.

*Note: Local PDF files stored directly on your device are never uploaded to our servers and remain on your phone until you delete them.*
